Regime-Dependent Value of Predictive Pre-Charging for EV-BESS Assisted Microgrid Resilience Under Myopic Real-Time Control

A co-authored research paper studying whether charging electric vehicles before an anticipated outage can reduce unmet critical demand in a microgrid supported by EV batteries and stationary battery storage.

Research question

How much can predictive EV pre-charging improve critical-load support during a grid outage when the microgrid controller makes real-time decisions using only current system information?

Strategies compared

  • Greedy heuristic dispatch
  • Optimized myopic dispatch
  • Predictive EV pre-charging combined with optimized dispatch

Evaluation method

The study uses a simulation framework with varying storage availability, EV participation, and outage duration. Energy Not Served (ENS) is the main resilience metric, and an energy adequacy ratio characterizes storage relative to critical demand.

Main finding

The paper reports lower ENS with predictive pre-charging across the evaluated scenarios. The size of the benefit depends on storage availability, EV fleet participation, and outage duration.

Authorship

Muntasir Mahmud is listed as the third author. The work is a co-authored simulation-based research study; this page summarizes the paper and does not imply that all modelling tasks were completed individually.
